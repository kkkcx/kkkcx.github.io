    <h2 id="si-about-title">Researching the social layer of intelligence.</h2>
    <p>Hi, I am a Ph.D. student at <a href="https://www.ut-vision.org/">ut-vision Lab</a>, the University of Tokyo, supervised by Prof. <a href="https://sites.google.com/ut-vision.org/ysato/">Yoichi Sato</a>. I received my M.E. degree from the School of Artificial Intelligence, Beihang University in 2025, under the guidance of Prof. <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/xingxingwei1988/">Xingxing Wei</a> (<a href="https://rose-vision.github.io/">ROSE Vision Lab</a>).</p>
    <p>During my master's studies, I was co-supervised by Prof. <a href="https://www.suhangss.me/">Hang Su</a> and A/Prof. <a href="https://ml.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/~yinpeng/">Yinpeng Dong</a> at Tsinghua University (<a href="https://ml.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/">TSAIL</a>). Previously, I received my B.E. degree from the School of Computer Science at Sichuan University in 2022.</p>
    <p>I have also worked on autonomous driving, trustworthy AI, and generative models.</p>
